## 🏗️ PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE — 9 CORE MODULES

### Module 1: Branded E-Commerce Storefront
- Every wholesaler gets their own branded website + mobile app
- Retailers browse catalog, check credit status, place orders 24/7
- Custom subdomain (rajeshtraders.ecombharat.in) or custom domain
- No coding. No ₹2-lakh website bill. Live in 30 minutes.
- **Status:** MVP live (web app) — Android app in development

### Module 2: Expense Tracking
- Tagline: **"Save Time. Not Receipts."**
- Snap expense, auto-categorize, GST-tagged
- Eliminates manual petty cash tracking
- CA-friendly export format

### Module 3: AI Credit Intelligence ⭐ (Primary Differentiator)
- Real-time credit limit monitoring per retailer
- **Risk scoring system:** 🟢 Healthy → 🟡 Watch → 🟠 Risk → 🔴 Alert
- Instant WhatsApp alert when retailer crosses credit limit
- Predictive default detection (21 days before defaults happen)
- Automated payment reminder scheduling
- **Result:** Prevents 3-8% annual revenue loss from bad debt

### Module 4: Warehouse & Inventory Management
- Real-time stock levels across multiple godowns
- Dead stock detection (60+ day idle items flagged automatically)
- AI reorder intelligence (optimal timing + quantity)
- Pilferage/shrinkage detection via stock variance analysis
- **Coming Soon:** Hardware + AI theft protection 🚨

### Module 5: Personal Analytics Dashboard
- Tagline: **"See Your Business Grow — At a Glance"**
- One-tap answers: "Is month ka profit?", "Top 10 retailers", "Worst payers"
- Beautiful charts, no Excel, no formulas
- Trend visualization: sales by product, retailer, geography, time period

### Module 6: Data Visualization
- Business growth charts
- Retailer risk heatmap
- Cash flow forecasting
- Comparative period analysis (Diwali this year vs last year)

### Module 7: Vendor & Retailer Network
- Verified vendor and manufacturer directory
- Smart sourcing: When stock falls below reorder level → surface 3 verified vendors with pricing
- Mediation fee per transaction (revenue stream for EcomBharat)
- Retailer discovery: Find new buyers in your region

### Module 8: Vendor Purchase Orders & Invoicing
- Raise PO to supplier in 2 taps
- GST-compliant invoices auto-generated from confirmed orders
- Invoice delivery: PDF download + WhatsApp share
- WhatsApp integration for all order communications
- E-way bill and e-invoice support

### Module 9: AICA — AI Business Assistant ⭐
- **AICA = AI CA (Chartered Accountant) Assistant**
- Trained on the wholesaler's own business data (strict per-tenant isolation)
- Answers in Hindi, English, and Hinglish
- Sample queries it handles:
  - "Mera sabse zyada baki kispe hai?"
  - "Kaunsa product 60 din se nahi bika?"
  - "Diwali ke time best seller kaun tha?"
  - "Who is at highest credit risk this month?"
- Daily morning briefing: "Good morning Rajesh-ji. Today you have 5 orders, 2 retailers need attention..."
- **Think of it as:** Digital Munim-ji — the wise business advisor in your pocket

**Q: What makes EcomBharat AI different from Tally?**  
A: Tally records what happened. EcomBharat tells you what's about to happen. Tally gives reports at month-end. We give alerts in real-time. Tally speaks to accountants. We speak to business owners — in Hindi.

**Q: Why not just use Khatabook?**  
A: Khatabook is a digital ledger — it tracks credit manually. EcomBharat AI watches every retailer 24/7 automatically, predicts defaults 21 days early, gives them their own branded storefront, and answers business questions in Hindi. Completely different category.

**Q: What's the pricing?**  
A: ₹500-1,500/month depending on plan. We don't mention pricing in content — handle in sales calls.

**Q: Is it mobile?**  
A: Currently a responsive web app. Android app is in development (Phase 2). iOS follows.

**Q: Does it integrate with Tally?**  
A: We're building the layer above Tally — you don't need to leave Tally for tax compliance. EcomBharat handles the intelligence, operations, and commerce layer. Integration roadmap is on the table.

**Q: What about data security?**  
A: Strict multi-tenant architecture. Each wholesaler's data is completely isolated. No wholesaler can see another's data. DPDP Act 2023 compliant.
